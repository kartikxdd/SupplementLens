What this site is (and isn’t)

It is

  • A category-aware comparison engine for gym & nutrition supplements
  • A scoring layer for dose honesty, fillers, sugars, allergens, and cost efficiency
  • Built to connect to Supabase later for real product data via API

It isn’t

  • Medical advice or a substitute for a clinician / dietitian
  • A storefront — no checkout, just decision support
  • Finished brand data yet — placeholders until your database is live
